About This Project

Problem: People may share misleading information or expose personal data because they cannot easily assess online claims and risks.

Solution: One learning hub combining a source-checking workflow, cyber-safety habits, scenario-based quizzes and a media-awareness assistant.

What makes it useful: It teaches a repeatable “Pause → Check → Protect → Share responsibly” process and gives users feedback as they practise.

Privacy: Quiz progress and checklist activity stay in this browser when local storage is available. Do not paste passwords, OTPs or private information.

Competition demo: 1) Explain the misinformation problem. 2) Paste a claim into Fake News Check and show the uncertainty-safe result. 3) complete a cyber-safety habit checklist. 4) take a quiz and show the score. 5) explain that the AI panel has an offline safety fallback and live AI requires configuration.

Validation plan: Test on at least one phone and one laptop; ask 5–10 people to try the checker and quiz; record completion time, confusing labels and errors; improve the interface based on their feedback.

Current limitation: Live fact-checking and AI answers need a configured server and API keys. Without them, this page must not claim that a story is verified or that a live AI answer was generated.

Password Safety

Create stronger passwords and protect accounts.

  • Use a long, unique passphrase.
  • Never reuse passwords across accounts.
  • Turn on two-step verification.
Remember: Keep passwords and recovery codes private.
📩

Phishing & Scams

Spot suspicious links, messages and online fraud.

  • Check the sender and website address.
  • Never share OTPs, PINs or passwords.
  • Verify requests through official channels.
Remember: Urgent prize or payment messages may be scams.
📱

Privacy Protection

Keep personal data and private information secure.

  • Review app permissions regularly.
  • Keep your address and live location private.
  • Use privacy settings on your accounts.
Remember: Share only information you are comfortable making public.
💬

Social Media Safety

Share responsibly and manage online interactions.

  • Accept requests from people you know.
  • Block and report harmful accounts.
  • Ask before sharing another person's photo.
Remember: Be kind and pause before posting.

Fake News Check

Verify before you share.

Is this news real?

Paste a headline, news link, message, or claim to begin checking.

What would you like to check?

A result should be based on evidence. Unverified claims must not be labelled as confirmed.

This educational checker helps you organize a source-checking process. It does not access live fact-checking services or automatically determine whether a claim is true.

How the result should appear

✓

Supported by evidence

Reliable sources support the claim. Show the source and date checked.

⚠

Misleading or contradicted

Explain what is misleading and provide reliable evidence.

?

Unable to verify

More evidence is needed. Never guess a result.

Before sharing, check these 4 things

  • ☑　Original source and author
  • ▣　Publication date and context
  • ▢　Evidence from other reliable sources
  • ▧　Whether the photo or video is out of context

Results depend on matching published fact-check records. No match is not proof that a claim is true.

Check more than one source

Use these tools to look for published fact-checks. A search result is a lead to inspect, not proof by itself.

  • Google Fact Check Explorer
  • PIB Fact Check (government claims in India)
  • Alt News
  • BOOM Live

Always read the article, date, evidence and original context. These sources do not cover every claim.
